## Speaker: Per Block (with Marion Hoffman and Claudia Noack)
### Institution: University of Zurich
### Date: 14:00 GMT, 12th November, 2026

**Talk Title**: Relational Alignment Models

**Abstract**: A common modelling problem is a situation in which there are multiple or many categorical outcomes and it is of interest how people sort into these outcomes (e.g. voting for parties, working in occupations, choosing a pub). Here we present an approach that allows to model the endogenous sorting of individuals into categories without resorting to (or in addition to) covariates that describe categories. Instead, it is modelled how much people whose attributes align make the same choices. The example application will be about the endogenous sorting of individuals into occupations based on gender, education, and other stuff.

**Bio**: Per Block is a quantitatively oriented Sociologist. His research focusses on modelling networks and network-type social systems with applications to the formation of informal relations, processes of contagion and peer rejection, and social mobility in its broadest sense. This work includes developing models that allow a statistical representation of relational approaches.
