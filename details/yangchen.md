## Speaker: Yang Chen
### Institution: Fudan University
### Date: 14:00 GMT, 7th January, 2027

**Talk Title**: Beyond Retrieval: Incorporating Personality and Emotion into RAG-Based Conversational Agents

**Paper**: [OpenReview](https://openreview.net/forum?id=A398Mkv0c5)

**Abstract**: Large Language Model (LLM)-based conversational agents are increasingly deployed in socially embedded scenarios, including personal assistants, intelligent vehicle cockpits, and companion agents. In long-term dialogue, agents need to retrieve prior context and adapt responses to users' evolving preferences and affective states. Existing retrieval-augmented generation (RAG)-based dialogue agents mainly improve memory access, but they often lack an explicit model of the user's current conversational state. We propose Psy4RAG, a plug-and-play RAG module that tracks inferred Big Five personality traits and short-term emotions in the pleasure-arousal-dominance (PAD) space. The resulting psychological-state cues are verbalized and used to guide response generation without modifying the underlying retriever or generator. Experiments on MSC and LoCoMo show that Psy4RAG improves response quality on average, especially on LLM-based coherence, consistency, and relevance metrics. On LoCoMo, Psy4RAG obtains average gains of G-con +7.29%, G-coh +7.23%, and G-rel +6.90%, suggesting that inferred psychological cues can help RAG-generated responses better fit the emotional context across dialogue turns.

**Bio**: Yang Chen is a professor of computer science and artificial intelligence at Fudan University, where he leads the DataNET research group. His work spans social computing, the analysis of networks, urban computing, networking systems, and AI applications in social science. Before joining Fudan, he held a postdoctoral position at Duke University. He earned his doctorate in electronic engineering at Tsinghua University and is a fellow of the British Computer Society and the Royal Geographical Society.
